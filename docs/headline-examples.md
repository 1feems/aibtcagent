# Headline Examples

## Purpose
These examples show what a valid newsroom-style headline should look like and what should be rejected.

## Accepted Examples
- `A newly deployed Stacks contract drew whale-sized first-use funding, signaling a live protocol launch before public dashboards catch up.`
- `Whale-sized sBTC funding hit a new Stacks pool because its contract received first-use liquidity onchain.`
- `A new protocol contract went live on Stacks after a first funding transaction confirmed deploy-to-use activity.`

## Rejected Examples
- `A newly deployed Stacks contract drew whale-sized first-use funding. This may be important for traders.`
Reason:
multiple sentences

- `There was a lot of exciting activity today on Stacks and many users appeared interested in a new pool.`
Reason:
too vague and not clearly provable

- `Liquidity increased.`
Reason:
no causality and no why-it-matters framing

- `Dashboard data shows a pool is getting bigger fast.`
Reason:
dashboard-first framing

## Rule of Thumb
A good headline:
- says what happened
- implies why it matters or why it happened
- stays one sentence
- remains short enough to scan instantly
